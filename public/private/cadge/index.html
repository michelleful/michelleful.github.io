About

Hello! You've found CADGE, an on-demand variant of OneLook's daily game CADGY. This version is for friends and family only, so please do not share far and wide.

How to play

Make 5-letter words using one letter from each column.

We start with a 3-minute speed round, during which you'll get 10 points per word found.

After 3 minutes, you can continue playing at your leisure for 5 points per word, and ask for hints (-3 points per hint).

Faded letters can't be part of any remaining word — only lit-up letters are still useful.

Please note that unlike CADGY, the target may not be 20 words exactly. Both British and American spellings are generally accepted.
